join, four source files, four LLVM files, four native reports and eight complete

The manually selected
[actual example](../examples/source_repeat_native_comparison_v1.json) is
1,122,815 bytes, SHA-256
`366fec40482151396b5328818b30a1c00258872323ff6c9bd99ba4d1670e2578`.
Its 23 decoded artifacts total 984,180 bytes. Six unit cases check this actual
example separately from synthetic fixture tests. It is never auto-fetched or
executed; named paths remain inert text.

The region is one MOV plus 1/2/15 ADDs, producing
`a.wrapping_add(b.wrapping_mul(N))`. Declared VGPR high-water 37 is separate
from encoded capacity 56 (O0) or 40 (O3), and the architected boundary 40.
Static unused scratch/input2 roles do not mean free or dead registers. Whole
entry instruction counts are not region instruction counts. Equal payload
bytes for fifteen/repeat do not merge their distinct case identities.

The separate importer caps raw input at 4 MiB before decoding, decoded artifacts
at 2 MiB, chunks at 64 KiB, receipts at 512 KiB, and each source/LLVM/report/HSACO
at 64 KiB. Structural limits are checked before expensive concatenation.
Failed or stale imports clear the previous view; there is no synthetic fallback.
These are parser/output bounds, not a claim about total browser RSS.
